Delete My Data

How to delete your Evos account and everything in it.

Last updated: October 14, 2026

You can delete a single note in the app at any time: swipe it to the left, or open it and tap the delete icon. To delete your whole account and all your data, ask us by email as described below.

How to ask

  1. Email contact@spacewiretech.com with the subject “Evos account deletion request”.
  2. Write from the Google account email you signed in with, or include the mobile number you signed in with.
  3. We confirm the request with you, delete the account, and tell you when it is done.

Ready to ask?

Email a deletion request

What is deleted

  • All your notes
  • Your account and sign-in details (name, email address, profile photo or mobile number)
  • Your subscription status
  • The link between your account and the app's usage analytics

What may remain

  • Payment records that the law requires us to keep, for as long as it requires.
  • Backups, for a short time before they are overwritten.
  • Your phone: notes cached on the phone stay until you uninstall the app or clear its data in your phone's settings.

How long it takes

We complete verified requests within 30 days.

Before you delete

  • Deletion is permanent. Your notes cannot be recovered afterwards.
  • Logging out of the app does not delete anything.
  • If you pay for a subscription, cancel the AutoPay mandate in your UPI app as well, so no further payment is taken.
