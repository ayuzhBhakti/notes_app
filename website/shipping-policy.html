Shipping and Delivery Policy

Evos is an app on your phone. Nothing comes by courier.

Last updated: November 2, 2026

Spacewire makes Evos, an Android app. We don't sell or ship any physical product, so there's no parcel to track and no delivery charge. This page explains how you receive what you pay for.

The short version: download the app, pay ₹3, and your notepad unlocks on your phone within a few minutes. There is nothing to ship.

What you are buying

You pay for access to the notepad inside the Evos app: a place to write and keep your own notes, tied to your account.

We don't sell or ship any device. Everything happens inside the app on your own phone.

How you receive it

  1. Download Evos free from Google Play.
  2. Sign in with your mobile number.
  3. Approve the UPI AutoPay in your UPI app. ₹3 is paid at this step.
  4. The notepad unlocks inside the app, usually within a minute of the payment.

Now and then a bank is slow to confirm a payment, and the app may take a little longer to unlock. If the notepad isn't open 30 minutes after paying, email contact@spacewiretech.com with your mobile number and the UPI transaction ID (UTR). We'll check the payment and switch your subscription on.

Monthly renewals

After the first 24 hours, ₹299 is taken by AutoPay once a month and your subscription carries on. You don't need to download anything again or take any other step.

Delivery charges and timelines

  • Delivery charge: none.
  • Delivery time: usually within a minute of a successful payment, and no more than 30 minutes in normal conditions.
  • Delivery method: online, inside the Evos app on your Android phone. Nothing is sent by post, courier or email.

Where Evos is available

Evos is made for people in India. You need an Indian mobile number, an Android phone running Android 7.0 or later, and a UPI app that supports AutoPay. Payment is Android-only.

Changing phones

Install Evos on the new phone and sign in with the same mobile number. Your subscription comes with you, and there's nothing to pay again.

Returns

Since nothing is delivered physically, there is nothing to send back. For money questions, read our Refund Policy. To stop future payments, read our Cancellation Policy.

Changes to this policy

If we change this policy, we'll update this page and the date at the top.
