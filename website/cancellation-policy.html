Cancellation Policy

Stop your Evos subscription whenever you like. Here's how, and what happens next.

Last updated: November 2, 2026

You can cancel Evos at any time. There's no lock-in period, no cancellation fee, and you don't have to give us a reason. Evos is run by Spacewire, and this policy applies to every Evos plan.

The short version: cancel the Evos AutoPay in your UPI app, and no more ₹299 payments will be taken. You keep the notepad until the end of the time you've already paid for.

What you are cancelling

When you subscribe to Evos, your UPI app asks you to approve an AutoPay for us. That AutoPay takes ₹3 on the day you start, then ₹299 once a month after your first 24 hours. Cancelling means stopping that AutoPay. Your account and anything you've written in the notepad stay as they are.

How to cancel

In your UPI app (fastest)

  1. Open the UPI app you used to approve Evos. This could be Google Pay, PhonePe, Paytm, BHIM or your bank's own app.
  2. Look for AutoPay, Mandates or Automatic payments. In most apps it sits under your profile or in the UPI settings.
  3. Pick Evos from the list. It may show under our company name, Spacewire.
  4. Tap Cancel or Revoke. Your app may ask for your UPI PIN to confirm. That's normal, because the request comes from your own app and not from us.

The cancellation takes effect as soon as your UPI app confirms it.

By email

If you can't find the AutoPay, write to contact@spacewiretech.com from any email address. Tell us the mobile number you signed in to Evos with. We'll cancel the AutoPay from our side within 1 working day and email you once it's done.

Deleting the app does not cancel the AutoPay. Logging out doesn't either. The AutoPay lives in your UPI app, so it has to be cancelled there or by us.

Cancelling during the 24-hour trial

If you cancel before your first 24 hours are up, the ₹299 monthly payment is never taken. You can keep using the notepad until the 24 hours end. The ₹3 you paid to start is not refunded. Our Refund Policy explains why.

Timing

Your UPI app sends you a notice before each monthly payment. To be safe, cancel at least one day before your next payment date. If a payment still goes through after you've cancelled, we refund it in full.

Letting your bank balance run low is not the same as cancelling. If a monthly payment fails, it may be tried again over the next few days, and your plan carries on once it succeeds. If you want to stop, cancel the AutoPay.

What happens after you cancel

  • No further payments are taken.
  • You keep the notepad until the end of the trial or month you've already paid for. We don't cut you off early.
  • After that, the notepad locks and the app shows the paywall again.
  • Your account stays as it is. If you come back later, your notes will be where you left them.

If you also want your account and data removed, follow the steps on Delete my data.

Coming back

You can subscribe again from the app at any time. You'll approve a new AutoPay, and the app shows you the price before you confirm anything.

If we end your plan

We may end a plan if our Terms of Service are broken. If we ever end your plan, or close Evos, for a reason that isn't your fault, we'll cancel the AutoPay ourselves and refund the days left in your current month.

Changes to this policy

If we change this policy, we'll update this page and the date at the top. You can always cancel under the policy that applied when you subscribed.
