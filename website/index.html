Features

Everything a notes app needs, nothing in the way

Open it, write, close it. Your notes are waiting next time.

Sign in with Google

One tap with the Google account already on your phone. No new password to make or forget.

Newest first

Your notes in one list, the last one you edited on top, each with its title, a two-line preview and when you changed it.

Write without fuss

A title and as much text as you like. Tap the tick to save, or tap a note later to change it.

Delete with Undo

Swipe a note to the left to delete it. Changed your mind? Tap Undo within six seconds and it comes back.

Works offline

Write on a train or in a basement. Notes save on the phone at once and go to your account when you are back online.

Only yours

Notes are stored in your account and only you can read them. Log out and they will be there when you sign back in.

FAQ

Questions people ask

Is Evos free?

Yes. Writing, editing and keeping notes in Evos is free.

Which phones does it run on?

Evos is on Android, from Google Play. There is no iPhone version yet.

What happens if I write a note with no internet?

It is saved on your phone straight away and shows “Saving…” in the list. When the phone is back online, the note goes to your account by itself.

Can I see my notes on another phone?

Yes. Sign in with the same Google account on the other phone and your notes are there.

Who can read my notes?

Only you. Notes are kept in your account and the server refuses anyone else who asks for them. We never send the text of your notes to analytics.

Do I lose my notes when I log out?

No. Logging out removes nothing. Your notes stay in your account and come back when you sign in again.

Can I organise notes into folders, search them, or add photos?

Not yet. Evos keeps it simple: a title and text, newest first.

How do I delete my account?

Write to us as described on the Delete my data page.
