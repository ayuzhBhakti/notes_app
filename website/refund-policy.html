Refund Policy

When we give money back, how to ask, and how long it takes.

Last updated: November 2, 2026

Evos is an Android app run by Spacewire. Subscribing unlocks the notepad inside the app: you pay ₹3 for your first 24 hours, then ₹299 a month through UPI AutoPay. Payments are collected for us by our payment partner, Cashfree Payments. This page covers when that money can come back to you.

If what you want is to stop future payments, you don't need a refund. Read our Cancellation Policy instead.

The short version: if we charged you by mistake, you get all of it back. The ₹3 trial, and a month that has already started, are not refunded.

Payments we refund in full

  • A double charge. You paid twice for the same trial or the same month.
  • The wrong amount. You were charged something other than the price the app showed you before you approved AutoPay.
  • A payment taken after you cancelled. You had already cancelled the AutoPay, and a monthly payment still went through.
  • Paid, but the notepad never unlocked. Money left your account and the app stayed locked. In most cases we can switch your subscription on the same day, and we'll offer that first. If you'd rather have the money back, tell us and we will refund it.

Payments we don't refund

  • The ₹3 trial. It pays for 24 hours of the notepad and sets up your AutoPay in the same step. It isn't returned, however much or little you used the app in that time.
  • A month that has already started. Each ₹299 covers one month from the day it is taken. If you cancel partway through, you keep using Evos until that month ends, but we don't pay back the days left over.
  • A payment taken while your AutoPay was still active. Uninstalling the app or logging out does not cancel the AutoPay. Your UPI app also tells you before each monthly payment, which gives you time to stop it.

If the notepad isn't opening, it's usually just a slow connection. Close and reopen the app. If that doesn't fix it, write to us anyway and we'll look into it.

How to ask for a refund

Email contact@spacewiretech.com within 7 days of the charge. Please include:

  • The mobile number you signed in to Evos with
  • The date and amount of the payment
  • The UPI transaction ID (UTR), the 12-digit number you'll find on the payment in your UPI app's history
  • A line or two on what went wrong

We reply within 24 hours. We will never ask for your UPI PIN, OTP or bank password, and nobody needs them to send you a refund.

How long a refund takes

Once we approve a refund, we start it within 2 working days. The money goes back the way it came: to the same bank account, through the same UPI app. Most banks show it within 5 to 7 working days after that.

We can't send a refund to a different account, in cash, or as credit in the app. If it hasn't reached you after 7 working days, reply to our email. We'll send you the refund reference number so your bank can trace it.

Failed or pending payments

Sometimes a UPI payment shows as failed or pending, but the money has still left your account. Your bank normally reverses this on its own within a few working days, and you don't need to do anything. If it hasn't come back after 5 working days, send us the UTR and we'll check it with Cashfree.

Changes to this policy

If we change this policy, we'll update this page and the date at the top. A change never applies to a payment taken before it was published.
