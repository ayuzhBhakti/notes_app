Privacy Policy

What Evos collects, why, and what you can do about it.

Last updated: October 14, 2026

Evos (“we”, “us”) is a mobile app operated by Spacewire. This policy explains what information the Evos app and this website collect and how it is used.

Information we collect

  • Google account details: when you sign in with Google, we receive your name, email address, profile photo and a Google account ID, through Firebase Authentication.
  • Your notes: the title and text of each note, and when it was created and last changed. A copy is also kept on your phone so the app works offline.
  • Mobile number: if you sign in with a mobile number instead, we collect the number to send you a one-time code by SMS and to keep your account.
  • Subscription and payment records: if you buy a subscription, we keep its status, the amounts charged, payment dates and payment references. We never see your UPI PIN or bank account details.
  • Usage and device information: which screens you open and which actions you take in the app, your app version, device model and operating system, an account ID, and where the app was installed from (for example a Google Play listing or an ad).
  • Advertising ID: on Android, the Facebook (Meta) SDK reads your device's advertising ID to measure which ads lead to installs.

What we never collect

  • We never send the text of your notes, your mobile number or a sign-in code to analytics.
  • We do not read your contacts, photos, files, location or SMS inbox.

How we use it

  • To sign you in and keep your notes in your account, so they are there on any phone you sign in to.
  • To provide and manage a subscription you choose to buy.
  • To understand how the app is used, find problems and improve it.
  • To measure whether our advertising works.
  • To answer you when you write to us.

Who processes your data

We use these services to run Evos. Each handles data only to provide its part of the app, under its own privacy policy:

  • Google Firebase (Authentication and Cloud Firestore): Google sign-in and storage of your notes.
  • Supabase: mobile-number sign-in, account and subscription records.
  • Our SMS provider: delivery of the one-time sign-in code to your mobile number.
  • Cashfree Payments: processing subscription payments by UPI AutoPay. Your mobile number is shared with Cashfree to set up the payment.
  • Mixpanel: app usage analytics.
  • Meta (Facebook SDK): app installs, app opens and advertising measurement.
  • Google Play: tells the app where it was installed from.

These providers may store data on servers outside India.

Who can see your notes

Your notes are stored under your account, and the database refuses every request for them that does not come from you while signed in. We do not sell your notes or show them to advertisers.

Sharing

We do not sell your personal information. We share it only with the providers above, when required by law, or with your consent.

Keeping and deleting your data

A note stays in your account until you delete it. Deleting a note in the app removes it from your account. Logging out does not delete anything.

To delete your whole account and everything in it, follow the steps on our Delete my data page. We complete verified requests within 30 days, except for records we must keep by law, such as payment records.

Your choices

  • You can ask us for a copy of your data, to correct it, or to delete it, by writing to support@evos.example.
  • You can reset or delete your advertising ID in your phone's settings (Google, Ads).

Security

Data travels over encrypted connections and is stored with our providers above. No method of storage or transmission is completely secure, but we take reasonable steps to protect your information.

Children

Evos is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect their information.

This website

This website does not use cookies or analytics. It loads its font from Google Fonts, which receives your IP address as part of serving it.

Changes to this policy

When we change this policy we will update it on this page with a new date.

Contact

For any privacy question, write to support@evos.example.
